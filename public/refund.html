Refund Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

1. Overview

We want you to be satisfied with CliperX. If you're not happy with your purchase, we offer a straightforward refund policy.

2. Lifetime Pro (One-Time Purchase)

We offer a 7-day money-back guarantee for lifetime Pro purchases. If you are not satisfied with the Pro features, you may request a full refund within 7 days of purchase.

Conditions:

  • Refund must be requested within 7 calendar days of purchase
  • We may verify usage to prevent abuse
  • Refunds are issued to the original payment method

3. Monthly Subscription

Monthly subscriptions are billed in advance for the current billing period. You may cancel at any time:

  • Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period
  • No partial refunds for unused days in the current period
  • You retain Pro access until the end of the paid period

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at hanhang789@gmail.com with:

  • Your order number or transaction ID
  • The email address used for purchase
  • The reason for the refund request

We aim to process refund requests within 2 business days.

5. Payment Provider Refunds

If your payment was processed by Paddle, you may also request a refund directly through Paddle's support. Paddle transactions are subject to Paddle's own refund processing timeline.

6. Exceptions

Refunds may not be granted in cases of:

  • Abuse of the refund policy (repeated purchases and refunds)
  • Requests made after the 7-day window for one-time purchases
  • Account suspension due to terms of service violations

7. Contact

For any questions about refunds, contact us at hanhang789@gmail.com.
